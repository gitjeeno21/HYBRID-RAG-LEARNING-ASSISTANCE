# Phase 2: Error analysis

Generated by `python eval/error_analysis.py` (zero LLM calls). Raw data: [error_analysis.json](error_analysis.json).
Live configuration analysed: hybrid RRF (k=60, 10 candidates per method), top 3, MiniLM, 1500/50 chunks.

## Where the misses went

| Class | Held-out v2 (47) | Legacy dev (50) |
|---|---|---|
| HIT_TOP3 | 41 | 37 |
| PAGE_SPLIT (a neighbouring page is in the top 3) | 3 | 4 |
| RANKED_4_TO_20 | 2 | 8 |
| NOT_RETRIEVED (not in the final top 20) | 1 | 1 |
| **Misses** | **6** | **13** |

Misses by v2 question type: paraphrase 4, exact_term 1, lookup 1. Multi-hop questions never fully miss, but only 5 of 10 get *every* fact into the top 3 (Phase 1).

## Findings

1. **Almost every miss was within reach.** For all 6 v2 misses and 12 of 13 legacy misses, the gold page was in BM25's top 20, and for 4 v2 / 9 legacy it was in dense's top 20. The live fusion only takes each retriever's top 10, so two v2 misses (v2_08 at BM25 rank 14, v2_51 at rank 18) are lost before fusion even runs.
2. **The cross-encoder reranker over the top 10 fixes 4 of 6 v2 misses and breaks none.** On the legacy set it fixes 4 and breaks 6, but:
   - 2 of those 6 (q26, q27) are artefacts of single-page labels. The answer sits in an exam table printed on pages 5, 6 and 30, and the reranker returned page 6, which contains the answer.
   - The other 4 are genuine losses (q38, q44, q45, q48), mostly short figure-caption pages that the reranker pushes down.
   - Net on legacy: about +4 / -4 genuine. On v2: +4 / -0.
3. **The page-label hypothesis is ruled out.** Every legacy label's page contains at least one expected keyword. The legacy labels are *incomplete* (one page when the fact repeats), not wrong.
4. **PAGE_SPLIT is common: 7 of 19 misses.** Chunks never cross a page boundary, and in these cases the retriever lands on the neighbouring page of the right passage.
5. **Embedding truncation is real but secondary.** In 12 of 47 v2 questions the gold quote starts past MiniLM's 256-token window inside its chunk, so dense search never embedded it. Dense ranks those in the top 3 for 8 of 12 (67%), against 30 of 35 (86%) when the quote is visible. Hybrid compensates through BM25, and only 1 final miss (v2_08) has its quote beyond the window. The sample is small, so this is suggestive, not proven.
6. **No gold quote is split across two chunks**, so the 50-character overlap is not causing misses.

## Recommendation: what to fix first (Phase 3 order)

| Priority | Change | Misses it addresses | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Larger candidate pool (20 per method) + cross-encoder rerank | RANKED_4_TO_20 + NOT_RETRIEVED: 3/6 v2, 9/13 legacy | Gold is already in the top-20 lists; the reranker has the cleanest held-out signal (+4/-0). Must also not lose on legacy. |
| 2 | Top-k 3 vs 5 vs 8 | Multi-hop coverage (5/10 all facts), PAGE_SPLIT | Cheap; more passages also cover neighbouring pages. Costs prompt tokens. |
| 3 | Cross-page chunking (keep the start page) | PAGE_SPLIT: 3/6 v2, 4/13 legacy | Directly targets the largest single class, but changes citations, so it needs care. |
| 4 | 512-token embedding model (e.g. bge-small) | Truncation: 12/47 quotes invisible to dense | Real effect on dense, small effect on the final hybrid list. Needs a ~130 MB model download. |
| — | Query expansion | — | No class points to it, and the reranker already fixes 3 of 4 paraphrase misses. An LLM-based version would also spend quota. Recommend skipping. |

Decision rule for Phase 3 (agreed in Phase 0): changes are chosen on the legacy dev set and confirmed on v2. A change is adopted only if it does not lose on dev and improves v2. Because the legacy labels are single-page, dev results will also be reported with the legacy keyword-based hit as a cross-check.
